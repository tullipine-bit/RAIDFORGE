Before you start

  • Use PP Max on Eruption et Water Spout.
  • Recommended stock: 5 Choice Specs, 5 Mouchoirs Choix et 1 Choice Band.
  • Add Black Glasses : they are used in Olivine City.
  • Starting setup: Choice Band on Weezing; Choice Scarf on Hydreigon, Blastoise and Vanilluxe; Choice Specs on the other two.

Why these stats?

Hydreigon's low HP and Defense IVs allow Weezing's Choice Band Explosion to KO it. According to the author, its special investment limits Surf damage to Weezing, even on a critical hit.

Weezing uses at least 25 Defense IVs for the Sableye + Seismitoad variation in Driftveil City. Vanilluxe uses 248 Speed EVs to outspeed the Aerodactyl in Violet City with Choice Scarf.

EVs are reproduced exactly as shown in the PokéPaste, including the values 6 and 10. IVs not mentioned are not required by this sheet. The PokéPaste explicitly lists Vanilluxe at level 100 and only gives three moves for Blastoise. Its HP/Defense thresholds are intended for future Explosion optimization according to the guide.
